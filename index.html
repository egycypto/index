ⓘ how this works
Connects directly from your browser to live Binance trade / order-book / candle data for btcusdt, blended with a rolling prediction market read for the near-term direction. The NET chip in the price row tracks trade-message rate against a rolling 5-minute baseline (LOW under 4 tx/s, NORMAL 4-7 tx/s, HIGH 7-10 tx/s, HEAVY above 10 tx/s) and feeds into every signal output on this page, same as volume, order-book and candle data — HEAVY and LOW, being the two extremes away from normal, are weighted more heavily in the combined call than an ordinary HIGH/NORMAL reading, since traffic that far outside the norm tends to line up with sharper price moves. A second, independently-learned signal fuses trade-count skew with a few outside price/momentum sources (CoinGecko, TradingView, DexTools) into one combined call every 30 seconds — shown above as the Multi-Source panel. Every trade updates the running totals the instant it arrives; nothing is sampled. The Buy/Sell Zone panel applies the core buy/sell rule: it watches price movement and data from every connected source (Binance live trades, CoinGecko, TradingView) inside a rolling window (15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or 1 day — set via the window selector), and reconsiders its call continuously as that window's data accumulates — when price has climbed toward the top of the window's range it leans SELL, when it has dropped toward the bottom it leans BUY. When the gap between the window's low and high widens, the call is flagged HOT or VERY HOT to mark how volatile that window has been. Learning tables live in memory for this browser tab only and clear every hour so stale tendencies don't linger — a page reload also starts them over. If any external panel shows "unavailable" or "no key configured", your browser or network is blocking that source, or it needs an API key to enable.

The Buy/Sell Zone panel keeps a rolling window of price data (15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or 1 day, set via the window selector) gathered from every connected source (live Binance trades, CoinGecko, TradingView), continuously accumulating that window's low and high, and reconsiders its call as new data comes in: price that has risen toward the top of the window's range calls SELL (price went up), price that has dropped toward the bottom calls BUY (price dropped), and the middle calls HOLD. The size of the gap between the window's low and high — shown as the "Gap" figure in that panel — decides how urgently the call is flagged: a wide gap marks the call HOT, a very wide gap marks it VERY HOT (e.g. "VERY HOT TO BUY" / "HOT TO SELL"), while a narrow, quiet window shows a plain BUY/SELL with no heat label. The displayed success rate blends everything else being monitored on this page: (1) how far price currently sits from the range midpoint, (2) that panel's own track record of scoring each past BUY/SELL call against what price actually did roughly 30 seconds later, (3) this page's Live-Data-Only signal report accuracy, and (4) whether the main 30s combined call, the live-data-only call, and the multi-source call currently agree with this direction — each of those in turn already factors in volume, order-book imbalance, 1m candle direction, Polymarket odds, network-traffic monitoring, transaction-count ratio, CoinGecko momentum, TradingView rating, and DexTools, so agreement or disagreement from those methods nudges this call's confidence up or down (see "backed by" under the call). A BUY is scored "correct" if price rose from the call price; a SELL is scored "correct" if price fell. Like the rest of the page, this is short-window trend-matching on an in-memory session, not financial advice. The Agent Bot section inside that panel acts on this exact same call: it paper-buys the first time BUY confidence clears its bar, then paper-sells on the next confident SELL — no real orders are ever placed. Every closed round-trip is scored win or loss, and the bot raises its own confidence bar when its win rate drifts low and lowers it when its win rate is strong, so it keeps adjusting itself to try to improve.
